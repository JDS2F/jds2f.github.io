Homelab Backup

This is a personal, non-commercial tool. It runs rclone on a few home servers I own to upload encrypted backups of my own files to my own Google Drive account.

It has a single user: me. It is not offered to the public, and no one else can sign in to it.

How it uses Google Drive: the app only reads and writes backup files in the Drive account of the person who signed in (the owner). It does not access anyone else's data.
